# Playtest checklist

## Game start: fire on port 1 starts a game in room 0; Bruce walks, jumps and kicks the same way

- [ ] At "room0_start", the rebuild looks and behaves as the original.
- [ ] At "walk_right", the rebuild looks and behaves as the original.
- [ ] At "jump_kick", the rebuild looks and behaves as the original.

## Application

- [ ] Start build/clean_loader.prg (the default build, protection off) in an emulator: the Datasoft title picture shows without the TBC text. A key or fire on joystick port 1 or 2 starts the game: the title screen appears with no cracktro before it.
- [ ] Play into rooms 2, 5 and 10 with the default build: the game does not hang and rooms 0, 1 and 5 keep their maps.
- [ ] On the title, press F3 and F5: the highlight moves between 1 PLAYER / 2 PLAYERS and COMPUTER / OPPONENT.
- [ ] Press L, R, shift-L and shift-R on the title: the line "JOYSTICK # n IS IN LEFT/RIGHT HANDED MODE" appears.
- [ ] Wait about 48 seconds on the title: the attract demo starts, and a key or fire returns to the title.
- [ ] Press F7 or fire on joystick port 1: a game starts in room 0; joystick port 1 moves Bruce.
- [ ] In a game press SPACE (pause message, SPACE again resumes), V (status line shows the credit text), F1 (back to the title).
- [ ] Take the lanterns of room 0 and leave the room; the next room is drawn and the score counts.
- [ ] With 2 PLAYERS and OPPONENT selected, joystick port 2 moves Yamo; when Bruce loses a fall the PLAYER 2 screen appears.
- [ ] Lose all falls: GAME OVER appears and the game returns to the title.
- [ ] The title tune plays on the title screen (every few titles) and the sound effects play in the game.
- [ ] The controls respond as fast as in the original.
- [ ] The animation is smooth and has no glitches.
- [ ] The music and the sound effects sound the same as in the original.
- [ ] The game flow and the level changes work from start to end.
- [ ] Nothing unexpected happens outside the tested scenarios.
